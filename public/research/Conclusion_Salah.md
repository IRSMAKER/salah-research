# Conclusion — The Quranic Meaning of Ṣ-L-W (صلاة)

**Source:** Verse-by-verse study of all 108 distinct Ṣ-L-W root verses (118 occurrences, 42 word forms) in the Quran, documented in `3_Study_Notes.md` (101 entries) and synthesized in `Final_Research_Salah.md`.

---

## 1. The Root's Core Meaning

The root Ṣ-L-W (ص-ل-و) names a **standing-state within the full working reach of a power that determines one** — sustained, renewed, settled. Not a momentary act. A state of being-in-the-reach-of.

Three grammatical proofs of "assigned lodging":
1. **Settlement-vocabulary** (fire-side): maṣīr (returning-settlement, 4:115), qarār (settled abode, 14:29), mihād (bedding, 38:56), ma'āb (return-hostel, 38:55), sakan (tranquility, 9:103).
2. **Assigned-to-him grammar**: جَعَلْنَا لَهُ جَهَنَّمَ (17:18 "We MADE FOR HIM Jahannam") — the lodging is grammatically assigned to the person.
3. **Worthiness-of-possession grammar**: أَوْلَىٰ بِهَا صِلِيًّا (19:70 "most worthy of being ṣ-l-w-ed in her") — the fire is a property of the worthy.

## 2. The Three Poles

| Pole | Meaning | Lexeme | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SITE** | space assigned to standing with the Name | ṢALAWĀT, MUSSALLĀ, MIḤRĀB | 22:40, 2:125, 3:39 |
| **STATE** | assigned standing kept up willingly | ṢALĀH, ṢALAWĀT (renewed cycles) | B/C/D/E clusters |
| **DELIVERY** | assigned standing, imposed | fire-verbs (yaslawnaha, nusliehi, saliyyan, asluwha, yusla, tasliya, salwah) | A-cluster |

The **noun ṣalāh** = the STATE pole, willingly kept. The **fire-usage** = the DELIVERY pole, imposed. The **site-words** = the SITE pole. Same stem, three directions.

---

## 3. The Same Stem, Two Directions

**Toward God (voluntary):** fa-ṣallā (87:15) — "he mentioned the Name of his Lord, THEN STOOD." The human stands toward the Mentioned Name, after purification and Name-mention. The divine ṢALĀH (B-cluster) is God's RESPONSE: He s-l-l on the stander, bringing him out of darkness into light, with angels as co-agents, ending in SALĀM-greeting.

**Toward fire (imposed):** y-u-ṣlā (84:12, 87:12, 88:4, 111:3) — the person is STOOD by the fire, after his own deny-and-turn (4:115, 58:8, 92:16). The fire-ṣalāh is the self-settlement of self-steering.

**The bridge (A19, 87:15):** fa-ṣallā is the HINGE. The same stem, object-free, aimed at the Mentioned Name. The fire-side always took a fire-phrase as object (yaslawnaha, yusla sa'iran, yasla an-nara al-kubra, nusliehi jahannama). The God-side is object-free: the direction is fixed by the preceding clause (he mentioned the Name of his Lord, THEN STOOD). The stem does not change. The DIRECTION-GIVER is the Mentioned Name.

## 4. The Breach-Pattern (Self-Steering → Self-Settlement)

In EVERY fire-usage, the delivered person first steered himself:

| Verse | Self-steering | Fire-delivery |
|---|---|---|
| 4:10 | consumed orphans' property unjustly | swallow fire in belly |
| 4:115 | defied Messenger + left believers' course | ṣ-l-w into Jahannam |
| 17:18 | wanted the immediate (ajl) | assigned Jahannam |
| 19:59 | lost ṢALĀH + followed desires | ṣalāh-loss → fire |
| 92:15 | belied and turned away | only the ashqā enters |
| 58:8 | whispered "why would God punish us?" | Jahannam suffices them |

The fire-ṣalāh is ALWAYS the END-STATE OF SELF-CHOSEN MISDIRECTION. The ṢALĀH (God-side) is the SAME relational structure but with the direction chosen rightly toward God.

## 5. The Honor-Economy

| Negative (fire-side) | Positive (God-side) |
|---|---|
| مَذْمُوم (blameworthy, A6) | honored (B2-B7) |
| مَدْحُور (driven-off, A6) | brought out of darkness into light (B3) |
| la'nah (curse, 2:161, 33:57, 9:84) | ṢALĀH (God's standing-toward) |
| la tuṣallī (withheld, B7) | ṢALAWĀT (granted, B2) |
| madhmum/madhur (expelled) | sakan (tranquility, B5) |

The ṢALĀH is HONOR that can be granted or withheld. The fire-stand is DISHONOR imposed. The same stem, opposite honor-directions.

## 8. The ṢALĀH as a State (Not an Act)

The root's native meaning is **to stand within the reach of a power**. The ṢALĀH is not a ritual act; it is a STANDING-STATE:

- **Innate** (B1, 24:41): the birds KNOW their ṣalāh by creation — not by ritual instruction
- **Granted** (B2, 2:157): God's ṢALAWĀT are GRANTED (not imposed) to those who confess return
- **Commanded** (C1, 98:5): "establish the ṢALĀH" = "make-stand the standing-state" (yuqīmū = to make stand/upright)
- **Established** (C6, 14:37): Abraham settles his offspring near the House SO THAT they may ESTABLISH THE SALAH
- **Maintained** (D19, 6:92): "those who believe in the Hereafter believe in it, and they MAINTAIN their ṢALĀH"
- **Guarded** (C16, 2:238): "guard the ṢALAWAT and the middle ṢALĀH"
- **Constant** (E9, 70:22-23): "those who are CONSTANT in their prayer"
- **Preventive** (E8, 29:45): "the ṢALĀH prevents from immorality and wrongdoing"
- **Transformative** (E8): the ṢALĀH is a standing-state that produces prevention

The ṢALĀH is both what you ARE (a standing-state) and what you ARE IN (a standing-state within the reach of God).

---

## 9. The ṢALĀH-Chain

Human act → Divine ṢALĀH → Exodus → SALĀM-greeting:

1. **Human act:** fa-ṣallā (87:15 - stand toward the Mentioned Name), or seek help in patience and ṣalāh (2:153), or spend as ṢALAWĀT of the Messenger (9:99), or repent (9:103)
2. **Divine ṢALĀH:** God s-l-l on you, with His angels (33:43), bringing you out of darkness into light
3. **Exodus:** darkness → light (the OPPOSITE of fire-delivery: into darkness)
4. **SALĀM-greeting:** "their greeting on the Day they meet Him is SALĀM" (33:44)

The ṢALĀH is always qualified by a soft-quality: raḥmah (mercy), sakan (tranquility), SALĀM (peace), taslīm (surrender), humility, constancy, prevention.

## 10. The Fire-Stand vs The God-Stand

| Feature | Fire-stand (DELIVERY) | God-stand (STATE) |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | Imposed (against the person's will) | Voluntary (toward God) |
| Quality | Holding (qarār, mihād, ma'āb, maṣīr) | Soft (raḥmah, sakan, SALĀM, taslīm) |
| Agent | God (nusliehi, yuṣlā) | God (ṢALAWĀT ʿalay) + human (fa-ṣallā) |
| Earned by | Self-steering (tawalla) | Purification + Name-mention + standing (87:14-15) |
| Outcome | Fire (Jahannam, Saqar, Sa'īr) | Gardens (naʿīm, Firdaws) |
| Honor | madhmum, madhur (expelled) | honored, brought out of darkness |
| Body | Skin roasted, replaced, tasted | Washed, rested, fed, heard |
| Time | Eternal (no absence, 82:16) | Renewed cycles (ṣalawāt) |
| Settlement | qarār, mihād, ma'āb, maṣīr | sakan (tranquility) |

The SAME stem, TWO directions. The root names a standing-state within the reach of a power. The power can be fire (delivery) or God (state). The direction determines the quality: holding (fire) vs soft (God).

---

## 11. The Three Surah-Bridges

**Surah 19:** 19:59 "they LOST THE SALAH and followed desires" → 19:68 "kneeling at Jahannam" → 19:70 "those who are most worthy of being ṣ-l-w-ed in her" (صِلِيًّا). The noun and verb of one root describe ONE standing-assignment.

**Surah 74:** 74:1-7 (standing-prescription: arise/warn/magnify/purify/abandon/no-favor-counting/patience) → 74:26 (sa-aṣliyhi saqar) → 74:43-44 ("we were NOT of the muṣallīn, nor did we feed the poor"). Within ONE sura: the ṢALĀH fire-assignment AND the ṢALĀH non-devotion confession.

**Surah 87:** 87:12 (the GREATEST-fire, passive-or-enter) → 87:14-15 (the SUCCESS-TRIPLET: purify + mention-the-Name + THEN-STAND, fa-ṣallā). The fire-side and the God-side are the TWO POLES of ONE standing-state in ONE sura.

---

## 12. The Settled Meaning

The root Ṣ-L-W (ص-ل-و) names a **standing-state within the full working reach of a power that determines one** — sustained, renewed, settled. Not a momentary act. A state of being-in-the-reach-of.

The **noun ṣalāh** = the STATE pole, willingly kept: a sustained, renewed standing-toward-God, qualified by soft-qualities (mercy, tranquility, peace, surrender, humility, constancy, prevention, sin-removal).

The **fire-ṣalāh** = the DELIVERY pole, imposed: a sustained, renewed standing-within-fire, qualified by holding-qualities (qarār, mihād, ma'āb, maṣīr, ḥamim).

The **site-words** = the SITE pole: space assigned to standing with the Name (ṢALAWĀT, MUSSALLĀ, MIḤRĀB).

The ṢALĀH is both what you ARE (a standing-state) and what you ARE IN (a standing-state within the reach of a power). The fire-ṣalāh is the same structure, imposed. The root's native meaning is **to stand within the reach of a power** — and the Quran builds its entire theology of worship, judgment, and transformation on this single standing-state.
