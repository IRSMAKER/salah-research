# Verification — Does "standing-state within the reach of a power" fit all 108 verses?

**Meaning tested:** Ṣ-L-W = a standing-state within the full working reach of a power that determines one (sustained, renewed, settled exposure). The SAME stem names: imposed-standing (fire-DELIVERY pole) and voluntary-standing (God-STATE pole), with assigned-space (SITE pole).

## Cluster A — Fire-Verses (DELIVERY pole, 22 verses)

The person is STOOD INTO the fire's reach (imposed). The fire determines them (burns, roasts, lodges them). The standing-state is WITHIN the fire's power.

**A1 (4:10)** — "Those who consume orphans' property unjustly... swallow fire in their bellies; and they will soon be scorched in a Blaze." ✓ The unjust consumer is STOOD IN the fire's reach (swallow fire in belly = lodged in fire's power). The fire determines them (scorched). FITS.

**A2 (4:30)** — "Whoever does that in aggression and injustice - We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire." ✓ The unjust doer is delivered-into the fire's reach (nuslihi naran = ṣ-l-w him with a Fire). The fire determines him. FITS.

**A3 (4:56)** — "We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire. Every time their skins are roasted through, We will replace them with other skins so that they may taste the punishment." ✓ The disbeliever is STOOD IN the fire's reach; the skin is replaced so the standing-state is SUSTAINED and RENEWED (roast → replace → roast). The fire determines them through total contact. FITS.

**A4 (4:115)** — "We will ṣ-l-w him with Jahannam." ✓ The defier is STOOD IN Jahannam's reach. The place itself is the exposure-agent. FITS.

**A5 (14:29)** — "Jahannam - they will be ṣ-l-w-ed with her." ✓ The people are LODGED INTO the fire's residence. The fire determines them (awful a settling-place). FITS.

**A6 (17:18)** — "We made for him Jahannam, that he may ṣ-l-w her, blameworthy and driven-off." ✓ The person is ASSIGNED a standing-state within Jahannam's reach. The delivered states (blameworthy, driven-off) are the honor-currency of the standing. FITS.

**A7 (19:70)** — "those who are most worthy of being ṣ-l-w-ed in her." ✓ The fire-stand is an EARNED TITLE (worthiness of being lodged in fire's reach). FITS.

**A8 (22:40)** — "ṣalawāt" (houses of Name-mention). ~ SITE pole: the places assigned to standing with God's Name. The meaning shifts from "standing-state" to "standing-site" — but the SITE is the space where the standing-state happens. The root's SITE-pole. FITS WITH NUANCE (site, not state).

**A9 (36:64)** — "ṣ-l-w her today, for what you used to deny." ✓ The denier is STOOD IN the fire's reach (imperative: told to enter). The fire determines them. FITS.

**A10 (38:56)** — "they will be ṣ-l-w-ed with her; awful a bedding." ✓ The transgressor is LODGED in the fire's reach (bedding = settlement). FITS.

**A11 (52:16)** — "ṣ-l-w her; be patient or not, it is the same for you." ✓ The denier is STOOD IN the fire's reach; patience is indistinguishable inside the delivery. FITS.

**A12 (56:94)** — "wa-taṣliyatu jahīm" (a serving of sustained roasting). ✓ The fire-stand is named with the NOUN of the root (taṣliya = kept-up roasting). The fire-stand is a NAMED standing-state within fire's reach. FITS.

**A13 (58:8)** — "hasbuhum jahannam yaslawnaha." ✓ The whisperer is STOOD IN Jahannam's reach as his sufficer. FITS.

**A14 (69:31)** — "thumma-l jahima ṣalwahu." ✓ The disbeliever is STOOD IN Jahannam's reach (salwah = deliver-standing). FITS.

**A15 (74:26)** — "sa-aṣliyhi saqar." ✓ The arrogant is STOOD IN Saqar's reach. FITS.

**A16 (82:15)** — "yaslawonaha yawma-d-dīn." ✓ The debauchee is STOOD IN Jahannam's reach on the Day of Count. FITS.

**A18 (87:12)** — "yasla an-nara al-kubra." ✓ The most-wretched is STOOD IN the Greatest Fire's reach. FITS.
